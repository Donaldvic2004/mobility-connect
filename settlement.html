Settlement and Resettlement Services

Guiding newcomers through every step of their settlement journey, from initial orientation to long-term integration. We provide personalized support to help you build a stable foundation in your new home.

Employment Aid

Connecting our community and newcomers with job opportunities, career guidance, and skills development programs. We help you translate your experience into success in the Canadian workforce.

Mentorship Program

Guiding our community and newcomers through personal and professional challenges. Our mentors provide wisdom, connections, and encouragement to help you navigate your new life with confidence.

Care and Mental Health Support

Providing support for care, emotional well-being and societal resilience support services to Canadians and newcomers. We ensure you have access to the mental health resources you need to thrive.
