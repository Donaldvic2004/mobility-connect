Who We Are

We are all immigrants

Immigrant Mobility Connect is a registered, not-for-profit organization providing a people- and person-oriented wide range of free services and programs to our community across Canada. Our programs are offered completely free of charge and are open to anyone seeking support.

We specifically assist individuals facing barriers such as homelessness, lack of access to education, employment challenges, mental health struggles, group or personal care needs, cultural and gender isolation, age-related hurdles, geographic isolation, language barriers, physical and cognitive disabilities, poverty, and discrimination based on sexual identity or race.

We provide a wide range of healthcare, mental health, and social services programs that are inclusive, equitable, and accessible to all. We are committed to bridging the gap in service delivery to address the complexity of social, emotional, economic, and health circumstances. Furthermore, we are committed to partnership with Indigenous communities, offering Indigenous-led programming and culturally relevant activities to serve diverse urban Indigenous populations.

Vision Statement

We envision a society where every individual, regardless of their origin, is fully supported to thrive—socially, economically, and emotionally. Our vision is a Canada where inclusive, compassionate, and comprehensive pathways to settlement, employment, mentorship, and mental well-being are accessible to all.

We strive for a future where systemic barriers are dismantled, diversity is celebrated as our greatest strength, and every newcomer feels a profound sense of belonging. By fostering mutual respect and understanding, we aim to build vibrant, resilient communities where immigrants and Canadians alike can grow together.

Ultimately, we see a world where the journey of migration is met with open arms, where potential is never limited by circumstance, and where every person has the opportunity to shape a brighter, more unified future for themselves and their families.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to empower individuals and families by providing integrated, person-centered, and culturally responsive support services. We are dedicated to fostering successful settlement, securing meaningful employment, providing access to dedicated mentorship, and ensuring robust mental health resources for those who need them most.

Through active community participation and strategic partnerships, we guide newcomers through the complexities of their new environment. We work tirelessly to connect people with the right resources at the right time, ensuring no one falls through the cracks during their most vulnerable moments.

We are committed to being a steadfast ally, equipping our community with the tools, knowledge, and confidence needed to overcome challenges, achieve economic independence, and contribute their unique talents fully to Canadian society. Every action we take is driven by the belief that everyone deserves a fair start.

Empowering Humanity Through Service

Overall, the organization's goals encompass a holistic approach to people's support, addressing various critical aspects including settlement, employment, mentorship, mental health, and community integration. This comprehensive approach is designed to contribute significantly to the successful integration of new immigrants into Canadian society.

The organization's focus on tailoring services to individual needs, culture, and faith demonstrates a profound commitment to inclusivity and a deep recognition of the rich diversity within the immigrant community. By understanding the unique backgrounds of those we serve, we can provide more effective, empathetic, and lasting support.
