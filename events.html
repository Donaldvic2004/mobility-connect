Making a Difference Together

Events & Programs That Transform Lives

From food distribution to community gatherings, our programs provide essential support and foster connection for newcomers and Canadians alike.

Services Offered

  • Food Distribution: Fresh produce, non-perishable groceries, and culturally appropriate meal kits are provided through weekly food banks, mobile outreach units, and emergency hampers.
  • Clothing Support: We distribute seasonally appropriate clothing for all ages, including coats, shoes, workwear, and children's items, ensuring warmth, dignity, and readiness for school or work.
  • Everyday Living Essentials: We offer hygiene kits, household items, baby supplies, feminine hygiene products, and other necessities that promote comfort, health, and cleanliness.
  • Emergency Support Packages: For individuals in crisis (e.g., fire victims, new refugees, survivors of domestic violence), we provide ready-to-go care packages containing essential goods for temporary relief and recovery.

Distribution Channels

  • Community Hub Pick-Up: Items can be accessed at our centrally located community center during scheduled distribution days.
  • Mobile Outreach Service: For those who are housebound or without transportation, we bring items directly to them through our mobile delivery program.
  • Pop-Up Events & Partnerships: We partner with shelters, schools, churches, and settlement agencies to host regular pop-up events that bring supplies to where they're needed most.

Our Impact

Since its inception, the Essential Support Program has reached thousands of individuals annually. Beyond meeting physical needs, our program helps restore hope, dignity, and a sense of community for those experiencing hardship.

By addressing the foundational needs of our neighbors, we empower them to take further steps toward stability, self-sufficiency, and wellness.
